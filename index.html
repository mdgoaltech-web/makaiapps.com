FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us a message — a real person will reply.

Do you build native apps or cross-platform apps?

Both. We recommend Flutter when one codebase can deliver a first-class experience on Android and iOS, and native Kotlin or Swift when an app leans heavily on platform features, background work or hardware. We'll explain the trade-off for your app specifically.

Can you publish my app to Google Play and the App Store?

Yes. We prepare store listings, screenshots, privacy and data-safety declarations, content ratings, signing and release tracks, and we handle review feedback until the app is live. The developer accounts stay in your name.

Who owns the code and the store accounts?

You do. Source code, signing keys, developer accounts, analytics and ad accounts are yours. We work inside them with access you grant and can revoke at any time.

Can you take over or improve an existing app?

Yes. We start with a technical and store audit — code health, crashes, performance, listing, reviews and monetization — then propose a prioritized plan. Many apps grow fastest from fixing what already exists.

Do you add AI features to existing apps?

Yes. We use on-device machine learning for speed and privacy, or cloud models where a task needs them, and design graceful behaviour for when a model is unsure or the phone is offline.

How do you monetize an app?

We pick the model that suits the product: AdMob ads with careful placement and mediation, in-app purchases, subscriptions with honest paywalls, or a hybrid. Every change is measured before it's rolled out widely.

What does a project cost, and how long does it take?

It depends on scope, platforms and how much growth work is included. After a short discovery call we send a fixed-scope proposal with a clear price, milestones and timeline — no open-ended hourly billing surprises.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Our maintenance and growth plans cover OS and SDK upgrades, store-policy changes, monitoring, bug fixes, new features and ongoing ASO and monetization work.
